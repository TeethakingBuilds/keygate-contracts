03Principles that shape every transaction

The operating model

Payment infrastructure should prove what it cannot do.

01

Cannot take custody.

USDC moves from the payer to the recipient—or into Circle’s official burn path. Keygate does not hold a pooled balance and never stores wallet private keys.

02

Cannot invent settlement.

The frontend is not an authority. Submitted, confirmed, attested, and completed states must be derived from Stellar, destination chains, and Circle Iris.

03

Cannot silently weaken policy.

High-value payments need the configured quorum. New recipients and bridge routes need explicit authorization. Every signed intent expires.

Before you sign

Clear answers. No shortcuts.

Can I connect my wallet and send today?

Freighter connection and direct native-USDC SAC transactions are implemented for Stellar Testnet. Choose the direct SAC path explicitly; it does not enforce Keygate limits or allowlists. Policy-routed payments and cross-chain burns remain disabled until the verified contract is deployed.

Does Keygate hold my funds or wallet key?

No. Freighter signs the reviewed transaction in the wallet. A direct payment invokes the official USDC SAC from payer to recipient. This page never asks for a secret key, seed phrase, or backend signing permission.

What is the fee?

A direct payment shows the exact simulated network fee in XLM before signing. CCTP route fees are fetched from Circle’s Sandbox fee endpoint and are labeled as quotes, not final signed maximum fees. No fee is invented when a provider is unavailable.

Is the destination USDC wrapped?

No. Circle CCTP burns native USDC and mints native USDC on a supported destination. Keygate does not issue a bridge token. Ethereum, Solana, and Base are the current policy contract destinations; Xebra’s other routes are not enabled in Keygate.

What if a transaction appears stuck?

Keep the transaction hash and query the ledger; do not resend blindly. A timeout is unknown, not failure. For a CCTP burn, query Circle for the real attestation and verify the destination transaction independently. Proof download is available here; destination claim submission is not yet enabled.
